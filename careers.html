01

Computer Vision Engineer

Engineering · Full-time · Harare or remote

Train, evaluate and deploy detection models on customer footage. You will own the whole path: dataset, training, threshold tuning, edge deployment, and the conversation with the plant engineer when the count drifts by three per cent.

PyTorch RF-DETR / YOLO ONNX · TensorRT RTSP pipelines
Portfolio required
02

Field Deployment Engineer

Operations · Full-time · Harare, ~40% travel

Camera audits, on-site installs, edge boxes, network troubleshooting and operator training. You are the person the plant calls when the alert stops arriving — calm, practical, and honest about what the system cannot do.

IP cameras Linux Networking Customer training
Driver's licence
03

AI Security Analyst

Security · Full-time · Harare or remote

Threat modelling for systems that call models, evidence work during incidents, and the kind of advisory that ends in a fixed finding rather than a forty-page report nobody reads.

Threat modelling Incident response Prompt injection Cloud & IAM
Writing sample

Research Scientist

Research · Full-time or fellowship · Remote-friendly

Turn site work into published method: evaluation under distribution shift, calibration for low-resolution safety cameras, and the measurement problems nobody has written up properly. You will publish, and you will spend time on the floor.

Evaluation Calibration Publications Fieldwork
Publications welcome
