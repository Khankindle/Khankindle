Photo-assisted outer-ear sizing for editable CAD. Manual landmarks, not AI. All image processing stays in this browser; no upload or account.

Bench models only. Do not insert or wear printed parts. A photograph cannot reveal hidden ear-canal geometry, validate depth, diagnose hearing loss, or establish a safe fit.

Choose a clear side-on photo with a 20 mm reference in the same plane as the outer ear.

Six landmarks

Use a helper, diffuse light, and the rear camera at about 40–60 cm. Keep the phone parallel to the ear. Include a measured paper scale beside the ear, never inside it.

  1. Reference start
  2. Reference end
  3. Concha bowl top
  4. Concha bowl bottom
  5. Concha bowl left
  6. Concha bowl right
Measure the usable inner bowl, not total ear height. Avoid hair and shadows. The reference must be coplanar; perspective error is not automatically detected.
