Logging and governance

This is a one-time read of a single export. It shows the shape of the leak, not a final number. Continuous routing, caching and logging turn it from a finding into a fixed number every month. If this looks like real spend worth recovering, book a consultation and we will implement the fix. The method is written up in The Quiet Leak (PDF).

What it looks for

01

Cacheable repetition

Clusters of similarly sized inputs to the same model — the signature of a large static prefix paid for on every call.

02

Tier mismatch

Premium-tier calls returning very little output, which often means the task never needed the top model.

03

Blind spots

Missing timestamps, unattributable rows and unexplained gaps — the things that stop you trusting your own numbers.
