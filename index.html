    What we won't promise

    Read this before the quote

    The limits are technical,
    so we say them early.

    A helmet at forty metres

    A 1080p camera covering a 30 m aisle gives about 18 pixels across a hard hat. No model fixes that. Bring us a still and we will do the arithmetic with you.

    Face recognition or identity matching

    We do not build it. Alerts point to a zone, a time and a clip that a supervisor reviews — never to a named person, never to an automatic penalty.

    Seeing behind a guard or into a closed machine

    Cameras see what is visible from where they hang. PLC and sensor data covers the rest; the useful system is usually both, wired together.

    Fixing a culture

    A camera everyone ignores changes nothing. If the shift team is not in the design, the dashboard will be muted inside a fortnight and the money will be gone.

    “Five of your forty cameras may be the right five. We would rather run a pilot on those than sell you forty licences.”

    02 / Advisory

    Security · implementation · cost

    The unglamorous work that keeps it standing.

    A model in a demo is easy. A model with an owner, an audit trail and a monthly cost review is the part that lasts.

    01

    Incident response

    When something the model did is already public.

    We contain, preserve evidence and get you to a defensible timeline — prompt logs, model versions, tool calls, retrieval snapshots. The familiar playbook stops at “reproduce the bug”; we work from the record instead.

    • 72-hour plan
    • Evidence preservation
    • Regulator-facing reporting
    02

    Cybersecurity advisory

    The attack surface moved; the framework didn't.

    Prompt injection, over-permissioned tools, keys in client bundles, agents with write access and no kill switch. We assess the system you actually built, then fix in priority order — with an owner and a date against each finding.

    • Threat modelling
    • Key & secret hygiene
    • Agent tool permissions
    03

    AI implementation

    Cheap to run, possible to hand over.

    Model choice, caching, routing, logging and the monthly review someone actually attends. We design for the team that inherits the system, not for the launch announcement.

    • Model selection
    • Cost governance
    • Observability

    04 / The studio

    Harare · since 2023

    Small studio. Unglamorous work.

    CloudyAI is an AI engineering studio in Harare. We started in incident response and AI security advisory, and kept getting the same question from operations teams: we already have cameras — can they tell us anything useful?

    These days most of our work is computer vision on existing plant infrastructure, plus the governance and cost control that keeps a deployment alive after the pilot. We are deliberately small: the person who audits your cameras is the person who trains the model.

    01

    The camera audit comes first

    If your footage cannot support the check, we say so before anyone signs anything. That is cheaper for everyone.

    02

    Video stays on site

    Inference runs on an edge box or your own server. Events and short clips leave the plant; raw continuous footage does not.

    03

    The shift team is in the design

    Alerts are built around what a supervisor can act on at 06:20 — not what looks good in a dashboard demo.

    Talk to us about a plant
